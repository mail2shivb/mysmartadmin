# 🎨 Accent Color - User Guide

## What is an Accent Color?

An **Accent Color** is a subtle personality touch that influences your app's appearance without overwhelming the content. Unlike a full background color change, the accent:

- Adds a **gentle tint** to the canvas (5-8% opacity)
- Influences **icon backgrounds**
- Appears in **selection indicators**
- Enhances without dominating

**Result:** Your documents remain the focus, while the app feels personal and premium.

---

## How to Set an Accent Color

### Step 1: Open Settings
Tap the **Settings** icon (⚙️) in the app bar

### Step 2: Find Accent Color
Scroll to the **Appearance** section
Look for **Accent Color** with the description:
> "Subtly influences icons and highlights"

### Step 3: Choose Your Accent
Tap any color from the 12 refined accents:

| Color | Best For |
|-------|----------|
| **Blue** | Professional, trustworthy |
| **Deep Blue** | Corporate, serious |
| **Green** | Growth, finance |
| **Deep Green** | Stability, wealth |
| **Orange** | Energy, creativity |
| **Deep Orange** | Bold, modern |
| **Purple** | Creative, unique |
| **Deep Purple** | Luxury, premium |
| **Pink** | Friendly, approachable |
| **Deep Pink** | Passionate, bold |
| **Cyan** | Tech-forward, clean |
| **Deep Cyan** | Professional, modern |

### Step 4: See the Change
- The canvas gets a **barely visible tint** (5-8% of the accent)
- Icon backgrounds pick up the accent tone
- Selection indicators show your accent
- **Cards remain neutral** - pure white (light) or elevated dark

### Step 5: Clear Anytime
Tap **Clear** to return to the default theme colors

## Dark Mode Accents

Accents work beautifully in dark mode:
- **Canvas:** Charcoal (#0F0F0F) with 5-8% accent tint
- **Cards:** Elevated dark grey (remain neutral)
- **Icons:** Darker accent tints for visibility

**Example:**
- Light mode Blue: Barely visible blue tint on off-white
- Dark mode Blue: Subtle blue glow on charcoal
- Both modes: Premium, calm, content-first

---

## Tips for Best Results

### ✅ Do:
- **Choose accents that match your mood or work type**
- **Use Dark Mode accents in low-light environments**
- **Try Soft Gradient for a more dimensional feel**
- **Clear accent when you want pure neutrality**

### ❌ Don't:
- **Expect loud, saturated backgrounds** - Accents are subtle by design
- **Worry about readability** - Text always appears on neutral surfaces
- **Use multiple accents** - One at a time for cohesion

## Philosophy: Content-First Design

### Why So Subtle?

**Problem with traditional "themes":**
- Bright colors dominate the screen
- Text readability suffers
- Cards disappear into backgrounds
- Feels playful, not professional

**Our approach:**
- Canvas remains neutral (off-white/charcoal)
- Accent adds **personality without noise**
- Content (your documents) stays the focus
- Premium aesthetic like Apple, Monzo, Notion

### The 95/5 Rule

For every screen:
- **95%** = Neutral, calm, readable
- **5%** = Your accent personality

This creates a sophisticated, "expensive" feeling while keeping your documents center stage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why doesn't the accent make the whole screen that color?
**A:** We use accents at 5-8% opacity to keep the UI calm and content-first. This matches premium apps like Apple Notes and Notion, where personalization enhances rather than dominates.

### Q: Can I make the accent stronger?
**A:** The subtle strength is intentional for a premium, professional aesthetic. Stronger accents would compromise readability and the calm, trustworthy feel.

### Q: Do accents work with all themes?
**A:** Yes! Accents work with System, Light, Dark, Emerald, and Indigo themes. The accent adapts to each theme's personality.

### Q: Will my accent choice be saved?
**A:** Currently, accents reset when you restart the app (in-memory only). Persistence will be added in a future update.

### Q: Should I use an accent?
**A:** It's optional! Many users prefer the pure neutral default for maximum focus. Others love adding a personal touch with a subtle accent. Try both and see what feels right.
